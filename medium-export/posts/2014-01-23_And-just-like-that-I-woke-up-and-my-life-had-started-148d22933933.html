2014 And the goal to write every day.

I am 22 days late. If I was a woman this would have a complete different meaning. I am 22 days late with my commitment to write something, anything, everyday of 2014. In a way I am glad I am. I do not belong to that statistic of people starting resolutions on Jan 1st and quitting after just a couple of weeks. Besides this is not a resolution. It is something I need to do to clear my head and at the same time write better. I tought of doing this anonymously but I’m braver than that, and i really shouldn’t care if the one or two people that will read this judge me or not because of what I’ll write. I will also write in english. One of the main points is, after all, to get better at it. I will write for me.

Welcome 2014

Sergio
